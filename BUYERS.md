## What we sell

Evaboot: exports LinkedIn Sales Navigator searches and lists to a spreadsheet or CRM, with verified professional emails.

## Who fits

- good: people who lead or run sales, business development or growth (Heads, Directors, VPs, CROs), at B2B software companies of 51 to 5,000 employees.
- maybe: revenue-adjacent leaders (customer success, account management) who may refer us to sales.
- no: anyone else. A "no" is not added to the CRM.

## The first line

- One sentence under 30 words, for the opening of a first email or a connection request.
- Built only from their title, their headline and their company's description. Never invent a fact.
- Never guess anyone's gender: use the first name, "they" or the role.
